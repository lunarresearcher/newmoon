    What stays on you
    
          
    The bots handle forms and emails. A few steps need a human, and a few things can't be removed at all.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Verification calls
    Some sites call your phone and read you a code. Your bot pauses and tells you which code box to fill.
    Whitepages · MyLife
    
        
    ID and face uploads
    Face-search sites want a photo ID or a selfie. The bots never upload either, so these are yours to do or skip.
    PimEyes · Facecheck
    
        
    Card-first searches
    A couple of sites won't show results until you enter a card or start a trial. The bots never pay.
    Archives · Searchbug
    
        
    Public records
    Property and voter records stay public at the county and state, so sites can pick you up again. That's why Watch runs every month.
    VoterRecords · PropertyRecs
    
        
    Scam calls
    Do Not Call stops legitimate telemarketers. Scammers ignore it. Fewer listings means fewer lists selling your number, so calls drop over weeks.
    donotcall.gov
    
        
    Links that move
    Sites change their opt-out pages. Newmoon's added sites are marked, and your bot opens each link first. If one is dead, it finds the site's own opt-out and tells you.
    marked "check link"

    Before you start
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Is it really free?
    Newmoon is free, with no account and no tracking. The quick scan works in any Grok account. The full removal runs in Grok Bot, which is an xAI subscription. The $129 comparison is DeleteMe's yearly price for one person.
    
        
    Is this made by xAI?
    No. Newmoon is an independent project built on top of Grok and Grok Bot. It writes the plan and the prompts; Grok does the searching and the filing in your own account.
    
        
    Does Newmoon see my name or number?
    No. This page has no backend. Your plan and progress live in this browser's storage, and the prompt goes only where you paste it. Clear your browser data and it's gone.
    
        
    Why do I have to approve everything?
    Opt-out forms ask for personal details, and some sites use the form to collect more than they had. Your bot shows you each draft with your number masked, and you say yes or no. You can approve a whole site in one go.
    
        
    How long until listings come down?
    Some sites remove you within minutes of the confirmation email. Others take days or a few weeks. Ledger records the date each opt-out was filed, and Watch checks again on the next new moon.
    
        
    Can I run it for my parents?
    Yes, with their permission. Run it in their name with an inbox they can see. Newmoon's rules tell the bots to work on one person only and to ask before touching listings for relatives.
    
        
    Where does the site list come from?
    45 sites come from BADBOOL, the Big Ass Data Broker Opt-Out List by Yael Grauer, a volunteer project kept up since 2017. Newmoon restructures it for bots and keeps its CC BY-NC-SA license. On top of that, Newmoon adds caller-ID apps like Truecaller and Sync.me and other people-search sites; those are marked in the directory. Radaris and Rehold aren't listed because both are offline under a court order.
    
        
    I'm outside the US. Does it work?
    Mostly not. Nearly every site here is a US people-search site. Caller-ID apps like Truecaller work worldwide, so the quick scan and those removals still help.
